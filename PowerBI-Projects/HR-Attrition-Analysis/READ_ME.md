# HR Analytics Dashboard in Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI project delivers an HR Analytics Dashboard designed to help HR teams
monitor employee workforce composition, employee attrition, and workforce trends.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of employee demographics, department
distribution, salary slabs, job-role satisfaction, gender, age groups, and employee
experience to support data-driven workforce and employee retention decisions.

## Screenshot

![HR Analytics Dashboard](./HR%20Analytics%20Dashboard%20Screenshot%20-%20big.png)

## Objectives

- Monitor total and active employees across the organization
- Analyze employee attrition count and attrition rate
- Identify departments with higher employee attrition
- Analyze attrition across different salary slabs
- Understand employee satisfaction by job role
- Examine employee distribution across age groups
- Compare attrition by gender
- Analyze employee attrition trends by years of experience
- Compare employee headcount across departments
- Support HR decision-making through interactive data visualization

## Tools & Techniques

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query for data import and transformation
- DAX for calculated measures and HR analytics
- Data visualization and dashboard design
- Interactive slicers and filters
- KPI cards and analytical charts
- HR workforce and attrition analysis

## Key Analysis

### Attrition by Department

The dashboard analyzes employee attrition across departments, allowing HR
stakeholders to identify departments with relatively higher employee turnover.

### Attrition by Salary Slab

Employee attrition is analyzed across salary ranges:

- 0–3 LPA
- 3–6 LPA
- 6–10 LPA
- 10+ LPA

This analysis helps identify whether employee turnover is concentrated within
specific salary ranges.

### Job Role & Satisfaction

The dashboard analyzes employee attrition by job role and satisfaction level,
providing visibility into the relationship between employee satisfaction and
attrition.

### Age Group Distribution

Employees are grouped into different age categories to understand the
organization's workforce composition:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

### Attrition by Gender

The dashboard compares employee attrition between male and female employees,
providing an additional demographic perspective on workforce turnover.

### Attrition by Experience

Employee attrition is analyzed against total years of experience to identify
patterns in workforce turnover across different experience levels.

### Department-Wise Employee Count

The dashboard compares employee headcount across departments, including:

- Operations
- Administration
- Sales
- IT
- Marketing
- Human Resources
- Finance

## How to Explore

Open `HR Analytics Dashboard.pbix` in Power BI Desktop to review the dashboard,
interactive filters, slicers, KPI cards, and visual insights.

Use the department selection and age filters to interactively analyze employee
attrition and workforce characteristics across different segments.

The dashboard is designed for HR professionals, managers, and business
stakeholders who need fast visibility into workforce composition and employee
attrition.
